| **Impact** | 🔴 High | Race conditions on `List<string>.Add()` will cause data corruption or crashes under concurrent execution. `Directory.GetFiles()` blocks the calling thread in an async context, and loading entire directories into RAM risks OOM on large codebases. |

| **Remediation Effort** | 🟢 Low | Straightforward replacement with a properly dynamic buffer or `StringBuilder`. |
