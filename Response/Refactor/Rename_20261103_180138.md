## General principles applied

1. **Match the domain vocabulary.** Qdrant calls them `must`, `must_not`, `should`, `keyword`, `range` — use those words in the builder API.
2. **Verbs over nouns.** `MoveStage` > `Reorder`; `BuildOutputPath` > `GetResponseDirectory`.
3. **Avoid single-word generics.** `writer`, `request`, `response`, `sw`, `api` are too ambiguous in a codebase with multiple writers, requests, and responses.
4. **Consistent prefix/suffix.** `ParseCSharpAsync` / `ParseWithGenericParser` pair naturally instead of `GetCodeDocumentsAsync` / `ParseFile`.
5. **One responsibility per name.** `RagAnswerOrchestrator` says *what* (answer) and *how* (orchestrates), leaving room for `RagRetrievalOrchestrator` if one is added later.
