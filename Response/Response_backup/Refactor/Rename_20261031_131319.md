## 5. Inconsistent Verb Patterns Within One Class

**`QdrantFilterBuilder`** currently mixes three patterns:

| Current | Suggested | Why |
|---|---|---|
| `AddMatch(field, value)` | `RequireKeyword(field, value)` | "Add" is the *mechanism*; "Require" is the *intent*. |
| `AddExclusion(field, value)` | `ExcludeKeyword(field, value)` | Parallel to `RequireKeyword`. |
| `AddRange(field, gte, lte)` | `RequireRange(field, gte, lte)` | Parallel to the keyword pair. |
| `AddExceptKeywords(field, …)` | `ExcludeKeywords(field, …)` | "Except" and "Exclusion" are redundant; pick one. |
| `AddExclusionRange(field, gte, lte)` | `ExcludeRange(field, gte, lte)` | Parallel to `RequireRange`. |

This gives a clean **Require / Exclude** × **Keyword / Range** matrix:

```csharp
RequireKeyword   /  ExcludeKeyword
RequireRange     /  ExcludeRange
```
